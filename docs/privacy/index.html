Privacy

Updated 16 October 2026

The game collects nothing about you. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your settings, scores, streaks and achievements are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. They never leave it. You can erase them at any time with Erase my progress in the settings, or by deleting the app.

The message checker

A message you paste into the checker is examined on your device and discarded when you leave the screen. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app asks for no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or notifications. It uses vibration for feedback, which you can turn off in the settings.

This website and the web version

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no scripts or fonts from anyone else. GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security, as its privacy statement explains. The web version of the game keeps your progress in your browser’s local storage, on your device only.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to site@hotmail.com.
