Support

Something not working, or a scam you think the game should include? Write to site@hotmail.com.

When you write

Include the version shown under About in the settings and your phone model. A screenshot helps.

Suggest a card

Send the message that fooled someone you know, with names, numbers and links removed. If it teaches something new, it may become a card.

Quick fixes

  • No sound: check Sound effects and Music in the settings, and your phone’s silent switch.
  • Calls wait for a tap: turn on Play calls automatically in the settings.
  • Text too small: choose a larger Text size in the settings.
  • Start again from zero: Erase my progress in the settings.
  • Wrong language: change it under Language in the settings.

Accessibility

The game offers larger text, reduced motion, a light and a dark theme, and a written transcript for every call. If anything gets in your way, please tell me.
